# Proposal: Add order history

## Why
Customers ask support about their past orders; they should see them in their account.

## What Changes
- An "Orders" page in the account: every order, newest first, with its status and total.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `user-accounts`: the order history.

## Impact
- Front end: the "Orders" page. API: the customer's orders, paged.
